# Legendary Survival Overhaul Update! - v1.3.3
This updates is made to balance the Legendary Survival Overhaul mod to fits with the theme of Requiem as much as possible while going for more stability.


  
Changes and Fixes:

- Improved Performance
- Removed MmmMmmMmmMmm (Target Dummy), was causing performance issues
- Removed Reality Piercer Enchantment, was causing causing crashes (Goety Revelation)
- Removed Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template from Loot Chests
- Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template it can be obtained by killing Piglin Brute, 20% of chance to drop it
- Fixed too much Hot Temperature in Nether dimension (Legendary Survival Overhaul)
- Fixed too much Cold Temperature in End dimension (Legendary Survival Overhaul)
- Temperature and Thirst won't kill the player anymore (Legendary Survival Overhaul)
- Nether Fortress will no longer spawn in Basalt Deltas biome (YUNG's Better Nether Fortress)
- Modified Chapter IV: Forget Me Questline
- Modified Tips & Tricks Questline
- Created Crafting Recipes for Heat and Cold Resistance Rings (Legendary Survival Overhaul)
