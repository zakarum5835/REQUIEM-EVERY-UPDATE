# Changes to the Storyline! - v1.4.4
This update is made with the intention of changing a small part of the Storyline, specifically in Chapter V: A New God, since The Abyss dimension wasn't working properly anymore and I decided to remove it and replacing it with The Graveyard, which is more enjoyable and more smooth. There are also made new balances to the modpack, increasing some spawn rate of certain ores, like Diamonds, Daemonium and most Voidscape ores, fixing some bugs like JEI menu while going for v1.4.5.



REQUIEM MULTIVERSE IS COMING:
